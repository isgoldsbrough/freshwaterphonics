        What this is

        A weekly page made by a parent in the class, not by the school, and not reviewed by the school. Each Friday the school's update says which sounds and maths topic the children did and what comes next; this page turns that into about 15 minutes a day at home: 10 of phonics, 5 of maths.

        Nothing is stored and nobody is tracked. If the page is out of date, a notice at the top says so. If something is wrong, tell the parent who shared the link.

        How to say the sounds

        Pure sounds, short and clean. The usual slip is adding "uh": "buh" instead of b, "luh" instead of l. It makes blending harder later, so it is worth getting right from the start. The cues on this page are a reminder; the model to copy is school's own scheme, which has a free video of every sound: Little Wandle for parents.

        Five rules for the practice slot

        1. Sound it out, don't guess. If your child guesses from the picture, cover the picture and point to the first letter. Guessing works on easy books and stops working later.
        2. Short, and end well. Ten minutes. Stop while it is still going well, even if you have not finished. A grim session costs more than it gains.
        3. Read aloud every day as well, with a book your child chooses. That is for pleasure and words, not a phonics lesson. Read any hard word to them and keep going.
        4. Never make it a bargain. No "do your sounds and then you can have the iPad". It becomes a chore to survive.
        5. Talk. Understanding what is read comes from knowing about the world, which comes from conversation. Ask what happened, what might happen next, why.

        What to do when

        Your child guesses from the picture

        Say "let's check with the letters", point to the first sound, and blend it together. Praise the blending, not the guess.

        They get stuck blending

        Say the sounds yourself slowly with a gap, then faster, then say the word. Let them repeat it. Then try the next word; do not drill the same one.

        They will not sit down

        Do it standing up, in the bath, on the stairs. Play one game only. If it is a no, skip today and keep the page for tomorrow; one missed day costs nothing.

        They melt down

        Stop. The session is over for today, with no consequence. Reading time must stay a good time or everything else here stops working.
