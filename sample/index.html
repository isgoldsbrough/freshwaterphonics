Tuesday: the sound b

about 10 minutes

  1. Show the letter b. Lips together, then open. Keep it short, no "buh". Say it three times together.
  2. Quick check from yesterday: show h."What sound is this?"
  3. Your child says the sound, then the word. Then finds one thing in the room that starts with b."b, bed. b, bus."
  4. Play the game, then blend one word out loud."b, e, d: bed."

Tricky words: show, say, spot the tricky part, say again.
